About Kumbh Mela

Welcome to Kumbh Mela

Kumbh Mela is the largest religious and cultural gathering in the world. Millions of devotees, saints, and visitors come together here to take the holy dip and experience spiritual awakening.

This festival showcases Indian traditions, faith, and culture. Various rituals, discourses, cultural programs, and the atmosphere of devotion are the highlights of Kumbh Mela.

Popular Places

Ramkund Panchwati

One of Nashik’s most revered bathing ghats on the Godavari River, Ramkund is central to the Kumbh experience.

Shree Kalaram Mandir, Panchavati

Historic temple dedicated to Lord Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana, located in Panchavati.

Triveni Sangam

The sacred confluence near Ramkund, spiritually significant for ritual bathing and prayers.

Muktidham Mandir

A marble temple complex featuring replicas of the 12 Jyotirlingas and Bhagavad Gita inscriptions.

Janardan Swami Math

Janardan Swami Maharaj was a renowned Marathi saint and spiritual teacher from Maharashtra, India, who lived during the 15th–16th century.

Kailas Math

Kailas Math is a historic Hindu spiritual ashram and centre where Vedic education and spiritual teachings are offered.
